# 🌍 GeoMiner AI

### AI-Powered Mineral Exploration Platform

GeoMiner AI is an intelligent exploration platform that uses **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** to analyze geological survey data and predict potential underground mineral deposits.

The platform helps identify valuable resources by generating mineral predictions, underground visualization, drilling recommendations, and economic insights.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🧠 AI Mineral Prediction
- Analyze geological survey data
- Predict mineral probability
- Estimate deposit depth
- Calculate confidence & uncertainty
- Predict ore grade and economic value

Supported minerals:

- Copper
- Gold
- Lithium
- Iron Ore
- Nickel
- Silver
- Zinc


### 📂 Smart Survey Analysis

Upload and analyze:

- CSV datasets
- Excel files
- GeoJSON
- SEG-Y seismic data
- Geological maps
- Borehole data
- Assay reports


### 🌎 Geological Visualization

Interactive underground exploration with:

- 3D subsurface models
- Mineral zones
- Rock layers
- Fault visualization
- Geological cross sections


### 🎯 AI Drill Recommendations

GeoMiner AI suggests the best drilling locations with:

- GPS coordinates
- Mineral probability
- Estimated depth
- Risk analysis
- ROI estimation


### 📊 Exploration Dashboard

Monitor:

- Survey history
- Prediction results
- Confidence trends
- Economic analysis
- Exploration insights


### 🤖 GeoMiner AI Assistant

AI assistant helps users:

- Understand predictions
- Explain geological terms
- Analyze exploration reports
- Provide survey guidance
